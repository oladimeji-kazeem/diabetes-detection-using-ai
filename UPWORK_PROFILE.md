## 1. Project Overview (For Job Posts or Portfolio)

**Project Title:** HealthScan: AI-Powered Integrated Metabolic Health Platform

**Short Description:**
HealthScan is a cutting-edge health risk assessment platform that leverages Google's Gemini 3 Flash AI to provide real-time, multimodal risk assessments for Diabetes and Cardiovascular conditions. It connects patients, clinicians, diagnostic labs, and HMOs in a unified ecosystem for seamless healthcare delivery.

**Detailed Description:**
We are building **HealthScan**, a sophisticated ecosystem designed to revolutionize preventative care and chronic disease management.
At its core, HealthScan utilizes advanced AI (Google Gemini 3 Flash) to analyze user vitals, medical history, and lab results, delivering instant risk stratification for:
*   **Diabetes (Type 2)**
*   **Cardiovascular Diseases (Heart Disease, Hypertension, Stroke)**

**Key Features:**
*   **🤖 AI-Driven Insights:** Real-time analysis with context-aware outputs (clinical jargon for doctors, simple language for patients).
*   **👥 Multi-Stakeholder Support:** Dedicated portals for Patients, Clinicians, Diagnostic Labs, Hospitals, and HMOs.
*   **💬 Interactive Health Assistant:** A built-in AI chat consultant acting as an Endocrinologist or Health Coach.
*   **📊 Comprehensive Dashboards:** Visual analytics for population health and individual patient tracking.
*   **🔒 Secure & Scalable:** Built with modern web technologies for performance and data privacy.

**Tech Stack:**
*   **Frontend:** React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS
*   **Language:** TypeScript
*   **AI Engine:** Google GenAI SDK (Gemini 3 Flash)
*   **Visualization:** Recharts
*   **Icons:** Lucide React
